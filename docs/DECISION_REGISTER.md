# Decision Register

Record important technical, evaluation, scope, ownership, resource, and organiser-rule decisions before acting. This register starts empty; no decision or approval is implied by a blank row.

The proposer supplies the entry; the Team Lead assigns a unique `DEC-001`-style ID and accepts shared decisions. Use date and local time. Evidence must identify a source/organiser answer, commit, experiment, review, or artifact path. Distinguish assumptions from confirmed facts.

Status options: Proposed, Accepted, Blocked, Rejected, Superseded. Preserve history: a replacement receives a new ID and links to the superseded entry. Accepted decisions affecting the plan must also be reflected in `docs/MASTER_PLAN.md` and summarised in `docs/TEAM_LOG.md`.
