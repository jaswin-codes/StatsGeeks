# Team Log

Master chronological project diary. No work or results are pre-filled.

The Team Lead integrates concise updates from member logs at phase checkpoints, after important reviews, and at handoffs. Append entries in date/time order; preserve failed attempts and use a later correction instead of silently rewriting history. Each entry is seven short lines. Link decisions and experiments rather than duplicating their full records.

Use a real date/local time, named owner, and evidence such as commit + run/artifact path. Label outcomes as planned, blocked, starter reference, smoke/development, reproduced reference, or final audited. Never claim an unexecuted run, unmade commit, or unapproved merge succeeded.

## Entry template — duplicate below for each event

Time: [date, local time, timezone if needed]  
Owner: [member/name]  
Task: [one sentence; task/experiment ID]  
Result: [actual outcome and status, or pending/blocked]  
Decision: [DEC-ID, or none]  
Next Action: [specific step, owner, checkpoint/dependency]  
Evidence: [commit/PR, run ID, artifact path, review or organiser answer]
